Near Term
Stockton Diamond
Construction of a rail to rail grade separation between UPRR and BNSF in Stockton California.  This project will result 
in a major increase in network fluidity in the San Joaquin Valley, the elimination of freight interference between both 
Class I railroads, and an overall reduction of freight interference with vehicles and pedestrians in the corridor. 
Installation of crossovers south of the UPRR Stockton yard to increase network fluidity during and after the 
construction of the Stockton Wye.  Restores and improves the connection between the UPRR Fresno and Oakland 
Subdivisions to the Port of Stockton.
